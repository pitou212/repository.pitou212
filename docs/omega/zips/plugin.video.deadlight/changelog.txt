[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.6[/B][/COLOR]
- MyAnimeList: sign in with your own MAL app, and episodes you watch update your MAL progress, as AniList already did. Rewatching an early episode never lowers your progress on either list.
- My Content has MyAnimeList Lists (Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to Watch).
- The Anime List Manager (was AniList Manager) sets status, score or removal on AniList and MyAnimeList together, and marks where the title sits on each list.
- The next page of a catalogue loads in the background while you look at the current one, so Next Page opens almost instantly.
- Continue Watching and Next Episodes show newly aired episodes on time again: air times from Trakt were being ignored inside Kodi.
- SIMKL only reloads widgets after a sync when SIMKL is your watched-status provider.
- Nyaa: batches are marked as season or show packs with a size per episode, and more English-titled packs are found.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.5[/B][/COLOR]
- Faster start: the Trakt, MDBList, SIMKL and AniList syncs keep their interval across Kodi restarts and never run while the home screen is first loading, so a restart no longer re-syncs everything and reloads every widget. The anime id index loads from a saved copy, and start-up skips work that never changed (add-on file check, skin files, update check when updates are off).
- MDBList list widgets now show a page at a time (your widget limit) instead of the whole list, with Next Page continuing in the add-on.
- Next Episodes and Continue Watching open about twice as fast: watched episodes are read in one go instead of show by show.
- Title search is much faster on a first search: results appear with TMDb artwork and TVDB / fanart.tv artwork fills in afterwards, and the detail lookups reuse a few connections opened while the search runs.
- On networks that block api.themoviedb.org, DeadLight now stays on api.tmdb.org once it has switched, and remembers it across restarts.
- Episode ratings, TVDB lookups and the MDBList / Trakt / SIMKL caches do less work while lists load; a dead TMDb host fails over in seconds instead of 20.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.4[/B][/COLOR]
- The Rows layout is now called Cards and was redesigned: a sidebar with the poster, title and the focused release's full name and tags, and a 3x3 grid of cards showing quality and size, tag chips, the release name, group and site, provider and cache state. The focused card zooms slightly, and each quality starts with a header ('4K - 27 results').
- AIOStreams results get their tags (HDR / Dolby Vision / HDR10+, codec, audio, channels) from the stream description. Remux releases get their own chip, ranked above Blu-ray, and names keep their '+' (HDR10+, DD+, Disney+).
- Release groups are coloured by tier in every layout, from TRaSH Guides (gold = tier 1, blue = tiers 2-3) and, for anime, TRaSH plus SeaDex best releases (pink). The group is now shown before the site.
- Pack releases read Season Pack, Show Pack or Pack. The List panel no longer leaves a gap under short names, and the cache state and provider are on separate lines.
- Much faster scrapes and results window: debrid cache checks start while providers are still searching, large cache lookups no longer fail on Kodi's SQLite (a rescrape of a big film went from about 6 s to under 1 s), and chips are built from cached tiles.
- Release names keep the dot in audio channels and codecs (DDP5.1, AAC2.0, H.265 instead of 'DDP5 1'), releases named 'DD+7.1' get the DD+ tag instead of DD, and 'DDPA 5.1' / 'DDP5.1A' (the A is Atmos) get DD+ and Atmos.
- A release several scrapers found is now one result instead of several: it reads 'COMET +2' on the row, and the List panel and Cards sidebar name every scraper. The copy kept is the one whose name tells the most. Filters gain 'Show from <scraper>', which counts every scraper that found a result.
- Quick-filter pills above the results: 4K / 1080P / 720P, Cached, HDR · DV and HQ Audio (Atmos, TrueHD, DTS-HD MA, DTS-X, FLAC). Press Up from the top row to reach them, OK to toggle, Back to clear. Qualities combine with each other, the rest narrow the list. The quality header now counts what is shown, and filtered lists start at the top.
- Release groups are read more carefully: language and format tags (PT-BR, WEB-DL, HDR-DV, Dual-Lat, Multi-Subs) are no longer shown as groups, and 'REMUX-FraMeSToR.MULTI.PT-BR' reads FraMeSToR.
- Fixed a Kodi crash on exit when DeadLight was still marking items watched in the background.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.3[/B][/COLOR]
- The results window was redesigned. List view has compact rows with a detail panel for the selected release: fanart, the full release name, its tags as chips, Site, Group, an estimated bitrate, and Video / Audio / Language rows. WideList shows the same information in columns, and Rows tiles match the List style. Release tags are now text chips (Dolby Vision, HDR10, Atmos, 5.1, MULTI) instead of logos in every view.
- DeadLight's own settings window is back (Kodi's settings dialog still works and stays in step), and the highlight skips the section headings. Logins for SIMKL, MDBList, AniList and TorBox show a QR code.
- Options > Enable Scrapers lists every torrent provider by name. New provider Torrents-CSV (off by default), and apibay is shown as PirateBay.
- Nyaa returns the right season's episodes (cour-aware matching) and keeps its connections open; it is still off until you switch it on.
- The loading window counts each release once and only what the list will show, lists what was hidden and why, and says when a film is still in cinemas (cam rips hidden until its digital release).
- Releases that are not the film itself are filtered out, and Blu-ray discs whose film is split over several .m2ts streams are skipped.
- Login links copied to the clipboard are no longer cut at the first "&", and AniList home rows no longer stay empty after a failed load.
- Tools > Check For Updates, Rollback and Online Changelog now use DeadLight's own repository; an update is only offered when the online version is newer.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.2[/B][/COLOR]
- New torrent provider apibay (The Pirate Bay), searched by title and fast (about half a second); it replaces Knaben, which was too slow. External Scrapers now lists Providers (by IMDb id) and Indexers (search by name) under separate headings.
- The loading window keeps providers in place while they search and sorts them once at the end, and each provider that found something gets a bar showing its share.
- The resolve screen's source block is easier to read: the service by name (TorBox), release tags in a fixed order with plain words (Dolby Vision, Atmos, 5.1, English), the release name in grey, and the source count turns amber when a fallback is being tried. The episode title is no longer in capitals.
- French releases (VF, VFF, VFQ, VF2, TRUEFRENCH) are shown as French, including in season packs.
- MDBList title ratings were removed; TMDbHelper provides them. MDBList lists and sync are unchanged.
- New installs start with the language invoker off (it could crash some boxes), and switching it from Tools can no longer empty the add-on's addon.xml.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 3.0.1[/B][/COLOR]
- Settings now use Kodi's own settings screen. MDBList Ratings and OMDb Episode Ratings finally appear there (Accounts), and a new OMDb Title Ratings switch lets TMDbHelper handle movie and show ratings instead. Plain API keys are shown instead of stars.
- The loading window was redesigned: it names the episode, shows one row per provider with its count, marks a provider that is switched on but not answering as "down", and uses three columns when more than ten providers are on.
- New torrent providers: Knaben, TorrentDownload, Bitmagnet, Meteor and BitSearch (all off until you switch them on), plus Test All Torrent Providers in External Scrapers.
- Fake releases are removed: a cached TorBox torrent that holds no video file (usually a lone .exe posing as a new film or episode) no longer appears in the results.
- Direct Streams no longer offers other episodes. HDHub was answering episode 1 with episodes 10-19 as well, and those bigger files sorted to the top.
- A provider is no longer shown as "down" just because the show has no IMDb id to search with.
- SmartPlay: the pin now follows each episode it plays, never plays a season pack for another season, copes with 4KHDHub names cut at 100 characters, and a pin can be cleared from the context menu (Clear SmartPlay Pin).
- Episode picking from packs keeps an episode whose title happens to be an extras word, and anime specials are no longer matched to a regular episode.
- Packs listed under one file name now show the real size of the episode that will play.
- Widgets reload once after playback instead of several times in a row, and stopping playback while Kodi is still opening a file no longer freezes it.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.12[/B][/COLOR]
- Drop Show now works when MDBList is your watched-status provider. It was sending the wrong kind of id, so MDBList matched nothing and the "Show Dropped" message appeared anyway. Toggle Dropped from the MDBList manager was failing too.
- SmartPlay: if your pinned source is missing from a scrape, the source list now opens instead of quietly playing a different release. The Next Up countdown still appears first when autoplaying, and the message explaining why the list opened now shows at the moment it opens rather than a minute earlier.
- SIMKL lists refresh far less. Watching one episode used to throw away every cached list and rebuild all of them; now only the lists that actually changed are refreshed.
- Episode scrapes no longer return files belonging to a different show with a similar name, such as Batman: Caped Crusader answering a search for Batman.
- Finishing an episode now records it as watched locally even if the update to Trakt, MDBList or SIMKL fails. The tick used to be lost on both sides.
- Watched Status Provider has moved from Content to Accounts, next to the Trakt, MDBList and SIMKL accounts it chooses between. The settings that open the General tab also have proper headings now instead of sitting under no heading at all.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.11[/B][/COLOR]
- SIMKL: finishing a cour of an anime now puts the next cour on your Watching list automatically. SIMKL files every cour as a separate entry, so until now the next one had to be added by hand once a cour ended. The finished cour is left alone - SIMKL completes it itself.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.10[/B][/COLOR]
- MDBList ratings no longer burn through the daily allowance. A title that has already been looked up is now remembered as done, so it is not asked about again every time you scroll past it. The full 1000 requests a day were going on re-asking about titles that already had their ratings.
- A title that already has its MDBList ratings is only re-checked once a month. Titles MDBList had nothing for are still retried daily.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.9[/B][/COLOR]
- SmartPlay: when your pinned source is found but will not play, the source list now opens instead of quietly playing a different release. This also applies while autoplaying the next episode, where it previously moved on without asking.
- Unchanged: if the pinned source is simply missing from a scrape, the best match still plays. A release being absent from one run is usually temporary; one that is present and will not resolve is not.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.8[/B][/COLOR]
- The TVDB API key has its own heading again. Moving the Artwork Provider settings out left it sitting under Fanart.tv, where it looked like a Fanart.tv setting.
- All Providers and Calendar have moved from Accounts to Content. Neither holds credentials; they decide how lists and the calendar are shown.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.7[/B][/COLOR]
- The Artwork Provider choices for Movies, TV Shows and Anime have moved from Accounts to Features, next to Ratings Posters. Accounts now holds only the TVDB API key, which is what actually belongs there.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.6[/B][/COLOR]
- Clearlogos now follow the Artwork Provider setting, like backdrops already did. Picking a TVDB option uses TVDB's logo; fanart.tv stays the choice for "Most Liked". Nothing loses a logo it had before, because every option still falls back through the others.
- Each cour of an anime season now gets its own season poster instead of them all sharing the first one. Re:ZERO is a single TMDb season covering four cours, so all four were showing the same image.
- Season posters now work with the "Most Liked (fanart.tv)" provider too. fanart.tv has no season posters, so that setting was quietly turning them off rather than sourcing them elsewhere.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.5[/B][/COLOR]
- Fixes episode Trakt ratings that were stored in the wrong scale by 2.0.3 and showed as, for example, 83 instead of 8.3. Affected seasons are refreshed the next time you open them; the rest are left alone.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.4[/B][/COLOR]
- MDBList ratings are now requested at a steady pace instead of all at once. Building a large list fired dozens of requests in the same second, MDBList rejected most of them, and the titles it rejected were cached with no ratings at all for months afterwards - while the daily allowance sat almost entirely unused.
- More of a list now comes back with Trakt, MyAnimeList, Letterboxd and MDBList scores on the first pass. A big list takes a little longer to finish filling them in.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.3[/B][/COLOR]
- Episode rows now show a Trakt rating, fetched for each episode instead of repeating the show's score. OMDb, the only per-episode source until now, rates 13% of this library's cached episodes and has nothing at all for shows like JoJo's Bizarre Adventure; Trakt rates 92% of them.
- The IMDb chip on an episode is unchanged and still comes from OMDb, so where both sources have a value you see both.
- To show it, set one of the three episode rating slots to Trakt in the skin's rating settings.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.2[/B][/COLOR]
- Season packs are now offered for a finished cour of an anime that is still airing. A show like Re:ZERO is one 85-episode season on TMDb covering four cours, so while the newest cour airs, packs were switched off for the whole show and the 2016 Blu-ray boxsets could not be found. Anime only; other TV is unchanged.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.1[/B][/COLOR]
- SmartPlay: a source is only pinned once it actually plays. Choosing a source that then failed used to pin it anyway, so SmartPlay reached for that dead release on every following episode until the pin was cleared by hand.
- SmartPlay: the pin now records the source that really played. When a chosen source failed and a later one played instead, the failed one was being pinned.
- SmartPlay: if a pinned source is found but will not play, the background fallback now re-pins whatever plays in its place. A pin that is merely missing from one scrape is left alone, since it usually returns on the next.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 2.0.0[/B][/COLOR]
- DeadLight now carries its own version. Releases are numbered deliberately and the old commit-count suffix is gone, so this is 2.0.0 rather than 1.0.0.200.
- The changelog starts fresh here. Earlier entries belonged to upstream Fen Light, not to this fork.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Artwork[/B][/COLOR]
- New "Artwork Provider" setting, chosen separately for Movies, TV Shows and Anime: TMDb Default, Newest Uploaded (TVDB), Highest Rated (TVDB) or Most Liked (fanart.tv). It selects backdrops and season posters together.
- The fanart cycle now follows that choice instead of ignoring it. The provider you picked leads; the others only top up once it runs out.
- Up to 20 background images per item, raised from 8, which fills the artwork gallery in the info dialog.
- Season rows now carry background images too. They were the one row type that had none.
- Season posters now appear in EVERY episode list. Previously only anime showed them and regular shows kept the series poster on every season.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Ratings[/B][/COLOR]
- AniList scores now show on anime rows everywhere, not only inside AniList lists.
- Rotten Tomatoes audience, Metacritic and IMDb gaps are now filled from MDBList. OMDb returns none of these for TV series, and a placeholder was being mistaken for a real value, so those chips stayed blank on every show.
- Per-episode IMDb scores now backfill into seasons that were cached while OMDb was unavailable. Previously an empty result was kept for up to 182 days.
- MDBList gained a rate-limit breaker: a throttled response pauses ratings briefly instead of deferring the item for 24 hours.

[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Fixes[/B][/COLOR]
- Fixed a crash that emptied the episode list on any single-season browse.
- A failed MDBList list fetch is no longer cached as an empty list. Lists came back blank and stayed blank even after the service recovered.
- MDBList failures now say so on screen when you are browsing, and stay silent in widgets instead of stacking up notifications.
